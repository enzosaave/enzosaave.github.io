Biography

Biography

Ph.D. candidate at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias.

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. under the supervision of Montserrat Armas Padilla and Teo Muñoz-Darias at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC). My research focuses on understanding accretion processes in X-ray binaries.

The primary objective of my thesis is to investigate low-luminosity accretion phenomena in low-mass X-ray binaries. To achieve this, I employ a multiwavelength approach, combining infrared, optical, and X-ray observations.

I also have a strong interest in high-mass X-ray binaries and ultraluminous X-ray sources.

Anatomy of a BH-LMXB

Generic BH-LMXB geometry
Hot inner accretion flow In hard and quiescent states, the inner flow can be hot, optically thin and geometrically thick, with H/R ≈ 0.1–0.5. Thermal Comptonization produces the hard X-ray continuum, while mild misalignment may support global precession.

Superorbital variability in the quiescent black hole X-ray transient A0620–00

E. A. Saavedra, R. Alonso, T. Muñoz-Darias, J. Casares, M. A. P. Torres, M. Armas Padilla, J. F. Steiner, D. M. Russell, G. J. Escobar, F. García, F. Lewis, D. Mata Sánchez, P. Rodríguez-Gil.

Long-term ZTF, LCO and ATLAS monitoring reveals a robust 261.9 ± 9.4 d superorbital modulation in the quiescent black hole binary A0620–00. The active and passive optical states vary systematically with superorbital phase, and the cycle may trace retrograde nodal precession of the inner hot accretion flow near the thin-disc transition at roughly 0.13 times the binary separation.

2026 · Accepted in Astronomy & Astrophysics
