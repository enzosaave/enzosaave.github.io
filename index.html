Biography

Biography

Ph.D. candidate at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias.

I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. under the supervision of Montserrat Armas Padilla and Teo Muñoz-Darias at the Instituto de Astrofísica de Canarias (IAC). My research focuses on understanding accretion processes in X-ray binaries.

The primary objective of my thesis is to investigate low-luminosity accretion phenomena in low-mass X-ray binaries. To achieve this, I employ a multiwavelength approach, combining infrared, optical, and X-ray observations.
